# ADR 0009: Jobs — studies, readiness, logger booking and prices

Status: accepted (Phase 4, October 2026)

## Context

A QA Tech job runs from the client's PO to payment. Before field work, a team leader
checks by hand that the PO has come, the protocol is approved, site access is arranged,
and enough loggers are packed whose calibration lasts until the last day of the study.
Loggers are shared between jobs, and report numbers are typed by hand in each client's
format. Phase 4 builds the job up to the start of field work. Later phases move it on
from there.

## Decisions

1. **A study is a copy, not a link.** Each equipment item on a job becomes a study that
   copies the equipment's set point, limits, logger count, interval, duration and cycles
   when the job is created. Later edits to the equipment don't change a job in progress.
   The report number is reserved in the client's format at the same moment and stored as
   text. When the same equipment is studied twice in one month, the second report gets
   `-2`, so a number is never issued twice. A unique index on `lower(report_number)`
   enforces this.

2. **Readiness is calculated wherever it can be.**
   - Calculated from the job itself: PO, loggers booked, loggers valid, and masters.
   - Ticked by people: protocol, site access and previous report.
   - Created ↔ Ready follows the checklist automatically after every change.
   - Field work starts only from Ready, after the checklist is recalculated in the same
     transaction. A certificate could have been cancelled since the job became Ready.

   The rules live in a pure function (`computeReadiness`), so the board, the job page
   and the status change always agree.

3. **"Valid" means for the whole study.** A logger counts only if a certificate in force
   covers the first day and is still in force on the last. When it doesn't, the reason
   names the due date ("is due on 30.11.2026"), because that's what the lab acts on.

4. **Bookings are serialised per organisation.** Booking reads which loggers are free on
   overlapping dates, then writes. Two people booking for different jobs at the same
   moment would both see a logger as free. Booking actions therefore take
   `pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtext('job-bookings:' || org))`. Bookings stay after field
   work as the record of what was used. A job holds its loggers only while it is in
   Created, Ready, Field Work, Data Issue or On Hold.

5. **Prices come from a rate card, matched most-specific-first.**
   - A line matches on work type, equipment type and duration; a blank equipment type
     or duration matches any. An exact work-type match beats a fallback.
   - A plain mapping line also prices RH mapping.
   - The study keeps the price it was given at creation.
   - A custom quote replaces the rate card for the whole job.
   - Money is shown only to roles with `jobs.money`: admin, manager, team leader and
     accounts.

6. **Audit summaries stay readable.** Summaries list a few IDs and a count; the full
   list goes in the after-snapshot. `recordAudit` shortens an over-long summary instead
   of failing the save.

## Consequences

- The job's status column carries the full flow, from Created to Closed. Phases 5–10
  add their own transitions to `TRANSITIONS` instead of adding new tables.
- Equipment and users gained `(id, organisation_id)` unique keys, so the job tables can
  use composite foreign keys and never point across tenants.
